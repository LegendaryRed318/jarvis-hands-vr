JARVIS HANDS VR

Holograms you work with your hands, seen through your phone's back camera. Everything runs on this phone: the camera picture is never uploaded or saved.

How to use it
  • Start, then slide the phone into the headset with the front cover OFF, so the back camera can see.
  • Pinch a hologram to grab and move it. Pinch empty air and move to spin it.
  • Two pinches on it: pull apart to resize, twist to turn.
  • Two open hands spread apart: it comes apart into pieces.
  • Hold an open palm still away from the holograms: the menu (next, add, delete, suit up, glove, colour, screen, recentre, exit).
  • SUIT UP: the gauntlet wraps your hand and moves with it.
  • SCREEN: the video you picked below plays on a big screen. Pinch it quickly to play or pause, pinch and move to place it.
  • Turn your head: the holograms stay where you put them. RECENTRE brings them back in front.
  • Keep a light on and your hands in front of you, not at the edges.
Make it an app on your phone

Tap INSTALL AS AN APP above if you see it. If not: Chrome menu (the three dots), then Add to home screen, then Install. It then opens full screen from its own icon, with no address bar, and works without internet once it has been opened once.

Watch a video in the headset

Pick one before you start. Then, in the headset, open the menu and pick SCREEN. (Videos saved on the phone only: YouTube and Netflix cannot be put inside the headset view.)

Settings (for your headset)

The wide (ultra-wide) back camera sees more of your hands: if you pick it, set Camera width to about 100. A USB camera plugged into the phone shows up here too, if the phone allows it.
